What it computes

Three pricing methods run on the same inputs, so disagreement between them is information rather than something hidden behind a single number.

PRICING

  • Black-Scholes with all five Greeks — delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho — and a continuous dividend yield applied once, in the right place.
  • 200-step Cox-Ross-Rubinstein binomial tree, European and American exercise computed in parallel, so the early-exercise premium is reported as its own number instead of being buried in the price.
  • Monte Carlo, 10,000 to 100,000 paths, with Merton jump-diffusion and a drift compensator that keeps the simulation risk-neutral.
  • Newton-Raphson implied volatility solver for backing IV out of a market price.

PROBABILITY

Probability of profit is computed from the seller's profit and loss, net of premium received — not P(ITM). A short put that finishes slightly in the money is often still a winner, and the number here reflects that. Breakeven, expected P&L and the full terminal distribution are shown alongside it.

SIGNAL AND VALIDATION

  • GARCH(1,1) conditional volatility with a Kalman trend filter, fitted causally — no information from the future leaks into any historical bar.
  • Anchored walk-forward optimisation. Parameters are chosen on a training window and evaluated only on the window that follows it. Slippage and commission are modelled.
  • Half-Kelly sizing, volatility targeting and mean-variance weights for position sizing.
  • Variance risk premium measured per name as implied minus realised volatility, scaled to that name's own volatility regime.

Who this is not for

It is not a signal service and it does not tell you what to buy. It assumes you already understand options mechanics and want better numbers underneath decisions you are making yourself. If you are looking for alerts to follow, or you have never sold a spread, this is the wrong tool and you will not get value from it.

It is also not a broker. Nothing executes here. You take what the terminal gives you and place the trade yourself, wherever you trade.
